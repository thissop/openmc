## Claim Discipline

- Do not claim the first VMEC-based stellarator neutron source for Monte Carlo transport. VMEC-derived source routines already exist in the HELIAS/MCNP/Serpent lineage. The defensible distinction is a native OpenMC source built on the Peterson API pattern, with local magnetic-field direction available to a polarized emission kernel.
- Do not call the current shielding result an optimum, gradient, Pareto front, or demonstrated factor-of-five improvement. It is a coarse finite-intervention response study.
- Do not call one-period transport exact until it agrees with an identically normalized full-torus run.
- Do not say source-surface replay solved the runtime problem. The measured saving was only 11–13 percent at equal histories.
- Do not say attribution, scalar worth, or clearance is the material derivative. They answer different questions on different domains.
- Do not say the fifteen stellarator cases were fifteen wall configurations of one reactor. They were fifteen distinct QUASR equilibria under one common conformal-wall rule.
- Do not say fixed geometry components are already cached in every production build. The archived `build_step1.py` reconstructs magnets; caching the fixed components is the target optimization loop and must be verified before it is reported as complete.

### 5. VMEC Supplies The Three-Dimensional Plasma Coordinates

VMEC is the Variational Moments Equilibrium Code. It originated with the steepest-descent moment method for three-dimensional ideal-MHD equilibria described by Hirshman and Whitson in 1983.

VMEC supplies nested flux surfaces,

$$
\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{r}(\rho,\vartheta,\zeta),
$$

and the physical volume element,

$$
\mathrm{d}V=\sqrt{g}\,\mathrm{d}\rho\,\mathrm{d}\vartheta\,\mathrm{d}\zeta.
$$

OpenMC does not solve the equilibrium. A producer converts the equilibrium to the compact source map consumed at runtime.

### 6. VMEC Determines Where Neutrons Are Born And Their Local Field Direction

The source measure is

$$
\mathrm{d}P\propto S(\rho)\lvert\sqrt{g}\rvert\,
\mathrm{d}\rho\,\mathrm{d}\vartheta\,\mathrm{d}\zeta.
$$

The rejection-free cascade is

$$
p(\rho)\,p(\zeta\mid\rho)\,p(\vartheta\mid\rho,\zeta).
$$

After a cell is selected, within-cell jitter and trilinear interpolation produce position and local

$$
\widehat{\mathbf{B}}=\frac{\mathbf{B}}{\lvert\mathbf{B}\rvert}.
$$

Every sampled birth has unit statistical weight.

### 22. Searching For A Cheap Alternative To Neutronics (II/III)

Geometric headroom:

$$
G(\mathbf{w})=\sum_v
\frac{\lvert\sqrt{g}_v\rvert}
{\lVert\mathbf{w}-\mathbf{r}_v\rVert^2},
\qquad
\mathrm{PF}_{\mathrm{geo}}=
\frac{\max_{\mathbf{w}}G(\mathbf{w})}{\langle G\rangle_A}.
$$

Nematic coherence:

$$
Q=\left\lvert\left\langle e^{2i\psi}\right\rangle\right\rvert,
$$

where $\psi$ is the outward-normal orientation relative to the local field projection. The doubled angle makes the measure nematic: directions separated by $\pi$ are equivalent.

Effective three-dimensional shaping:

$$
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{eff}}=
\frac{\max_{\phi}\sqrt{\delta R^2+\delta Z^2}}
{\min_{\phi}\lvert\Delta_0\rvert}.
$$

This compares intrinsic three-dimensional excursion with the minimum source-to-wall standoff. Device 59509 is approximately 0.43. Values below about 0.15 are the regime where the second-order series behaves well; values near or above two indicate breakdown.

Clearance:

$$
c(\vartheta,\phi)=
\min_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{C}}
\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{r}(\vartheta,\phi)\rVert,
$$

where $\mathcal{C}$ is the full winding pack. Clearance is a feasibility mask, not a neutronic predictor.

### 28. Choosing The Search Strategy

Gaussian-process Bayesian search:

- Use approximately 8–20 smooth Fourier or hierarchical surface modes.
- Treat OpenMC as expensive and noisy.
- Use expected improvement or a constrained acquisition function.
- Re-evaluate promising candidates with independent seeds.

Empirical response-operator update:

- Estimate the finite response matrix $B_{\mathrm{FD}}$ from real material interventions.
- Solve a constrained minimax or smooth-max local step.
- Build the proposed geometry and rerun OpenMC.
- Update the operator when nonlinear error grows.

CMA-ES appeared in an early outline. It is not the current preferred strategy because direct population fitting to Monte Carlo noise is not adequately controlled.

### 29. Optimizing The Complete Coil-Response Vector

The base problem is

$$
\underset{\mathbf{u}}{\operatorname{minimize}}
\;\max_j R_j(\mathbf{u}),
$$

subject to

$$
\mathrm{TBR}(\mathbf{u})\geq\mathrm{TBR}_{\min},
\qquad
M_{\mathrm{sh}}(\mathbf{u})\leq M_{\max},
\qquad
t(\vartheta,\phi;\mathbf{u})\leq c(\vartheta,\phi).
$$

Keep the complete vector $\mathbf{R}$ because the limiting coil can move. Candidate engineering responses are peak magnet heating density, total cryogenic heating, and fast-neutron fluence.

### 30. Attribution, Worth, And Clearance Answer Different Questions

Attribution:

$$
A(\mathbf{x})=S(\mathbf{x})\psi^{\dagger}(\mathbf{x}).
$$

This says where histories contributing to a coil response were born. Its natural domain is the plasma.

Scalar worth:

$$
W_k=\int_{V_k}\phi\psi^{\dagger}\,\mathrm{d}V.
$$

This says where coil-important flux visits the existing shield. It is a useful diagnostic, but not automatically the derivative of replacing FLiBe with WC.

Signed worth can subtract a breeder penalty, but it remains an approximate local diagnostic unless connected to the material perturbation operator.

Finite intervention effect:

$$
B_{\mathrm{FD},kj}=R_j(\mathbf{u}+\Delta\mathbf{u}_k)-R_j(\mathbf{u}).
$$

This is the actual measured response change for the chosen material swap.

Clearance answers only whether added build can physically fit.

### 31. A Fixed Envelope Couples Shielding And Moderation

Replacing FLiBe with WC changes several mechanisms at once:

- Moderation before the shield.
- Tritium-breeding volume.

- The spatial distribution that reaches multiple coils.

Fast magnet flux can rise even when some integrated attenuation measure improves. An adjoint source back-projected to the plasma identifies birth attribution; it does not directly prescribe the best shield location because the domains and material operator differ.

## Is StellaratorSource Alone Ready For A Peterson-Style Paper?

Short answer: it is in publishable-method territory, but the source alone is not yet as complete a paper as Peterson, Romano, and Dunn’s tokamak-source study.

What is already strong:

- A native OpenMC `StellaratorSource` API rather than a point-cloud helper script.
- Correct sampling of $S(\rho)\lvert\sqrt{g}\rvert$ on a real VMEC equilibrium.
- Rejection-free conditional CDF sampling.
- Local $\widehat{\mathbf{B}}$ interpolation for polarized emission.
- Bit-consistent independent Python-mirror tests.
- Real-equilibrium volume, Jacobian, LCFS, marginal, and transport gates.

What Peterson’s paper has that a source-only stellarator paper still needs:

1. A completed upstream integration and runtime parity story, including final native C++ tests on the target OpenMC branch.
2. Grid-resolution convergence and quantified interpolation, within-cell jitter, and toroidal-seam error.
3. Runtime and memory benchmarks over representative equilibrium resolutions.
4. A comparison against a plausible naive or legacy stellarator-source method, with a downstream consequence such as wall load, detector response, or shielding tally bias.
5. Validation on several equilibria rather than one flagship QH case.
6. A clear novelty statement relative to the HELIAS/MCNP/Serpent VMEC-source lineage.

Recommended paper framing:

The strongest near-term paper is not “a VMEC source exists.” It is a unified OpenMC plasma-source paper combining:

- A native VMEC stellarator source.
- A shared local-field spin-polarized emission kernel.
- Axisymmetric analytic validation.
- Three-dimensional conformal-wall validation.
- A downstream tokamak and stellarator neutronics use case.

That package has a clearer scientific claim and a more Peterson-like validation arc. A source-only paper becomes competitive after the six gaps above are closed.
